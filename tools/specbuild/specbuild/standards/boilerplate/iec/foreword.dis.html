Foreword

IEC (the International Electrotechnical Commission) is a worldwide federation of national electrotechnical committees (IEC National Committees). The work of preparing International Standards is normally carried out through IEC technical committees.

Draft International Standards adopted by the technical committees are circulated to the National Committees for voting. Publication as an International Standard requires approval by at least two-thirds of the National Committees casting a vote.
