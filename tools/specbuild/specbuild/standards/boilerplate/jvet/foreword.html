Foreword

ITU-T and ISO/IEC have established a Joint Collaborative Team on Video Coding (JCT-VC) and a Joint Video Experts Team (JVET) to develop standards in the field of video coding. The organizational structure of these joint teams is defined in the Procedures for the work of the Joint Collaborative Team between ITU-T SG16 WP3 and ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 29 on Video Coding.

This document has been prepared jointly under the auspices of ITU-T Study Group 16 and ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 29. It may be published both as an ITU-T Recommendation and as an ISO/IEC International Standard.

The procedures used to develop this document and those intended for its further maintenance are described in the ITU-T Recommendations A.1 and A.8 and in the ISO/IEC Directives, Part 1. In particular, the different approval criteria needed for the different types of ISO/IEC document should be noted. This document was drafted in accordance with the editorial rules of the ISO/IEC Directives, Part 2.
